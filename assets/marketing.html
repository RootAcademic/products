3

Upgrade Your Poster to High-Resolution for Printing

To ensure your printed materials look clear, professional, and trustworthy, follow these steps:

1

Go to photopea.com

2

Click the top-left menu: Image → Image Size

3

Set the Resolution (DPI) to 350 dpi

4

Save the image

5

Print using a laser printer

Pro tip: A 350 dpi resolution significantly improves visual quality and increases scanning rates. Higher print quality builds trust and encourages more people to scan your code.

How It Works

Start earning in four simple steps.

1

Register & Get Your Code

Sign up for any of our services. You automatically receive a unique 6-character referral code that you can share with anyone.

2

Share & Grow Your Network

Share your referral code. When someone registers using your code, they become part of your network. As your network grows, you advance through tiers and unlock deeper earning levels.

3

Earn Multi-Level Commissions

When anyone in your network makes a purchase, you earn a commission. Higher tiers let you earn from more levels deep in your network. Commissions are credited to your wallet instantly.

4

Withdraw to Your Bank

No matter which country you live in, you can withdraw your commission earnings to your local bank account in your local currency with a single tap in the app. We support 50+ currencies and 80+ countries.

Multi-level earning: Your tier determines how many levels deep you earn. At Bronze, you earn from direct referrals (Level 1). At Diamond, you earn from 5 levels deep. The commission rate for each level is fixed — advancing tiers simply unlocks more levels of passive income.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the affiliate program.

1How do I join the affiliate program?
Every registered user automatically receives a unique referral code. Simply share your code with others. There is no separate application process — you are already an affiliate.
2How are commission rates determined?
Commission rates are fixed per earning level (e.g., 20% for Level 1, 10% for Level 2). Your tier determines how many levels you earn from. Advance tiers by growing your direct referral count or total commission revenue — whichever threshold you reach first.
3What is the difference between tier and level?
Your tier (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond) reflects your standing in the program and is based on your direct referrals or revenue. Levels (L1–L5) represent the depth in your referral network. A higher tier unlocks more earning levels. For example, a Gold affiliate earns from Levels 1, 2, and 3.
4When do I receive my commissions?
Commissions are credited to your in-app wallet in real-time as soon as a purchase in your network is completed. You can withdraw your balance at any time directly from the app to your bank account via international bank transfer, subject to the minimum withdrawal amount and processing fee shown in the Payout section above.
5Can I earn from multiple services/rooms?
Yes. Your referral code works across all services. Commission rates and tier requirements may vary per service — see the commission structure tables above for details specific to each service.
6What do referred users get?
Users who register with your referral code receive a 3-day free bonus added to their account. This makes it easy to promote — your referrals get an immediate benefit for using your code.
7Is there a limit to how much I can earn?
No. There is no cap on earnings. The more your network grows and the more purchases they make, the more you earn. At Diamond tier, you earn commissions from 5 levels deep with no upper limit.
